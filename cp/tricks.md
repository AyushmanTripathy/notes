## Key for a string

1. Prime factorisation
    - Get ascii value and multiply ith prime for all letters
    - No of primes needed will be 26 or 256
1. Mutiplication of equal length arrays of ints is unique
    - Can be applied for anagram of same length words

## Rotating a Matrix

1. Clockwise -> Tranpose and then reversing rows
2. Anticlockwise -> Transpose and then reversing columns
